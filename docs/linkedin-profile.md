## Overview (≈1,370 characters)

> JiXora is AI program delivery and accountability software for fitness businesses. Coaches, gyms, studios and corporate wellness programs use JiXora to give every member a personalised 90-day training plan and the daily follow-up that keeps them on it.
>
> Most members don't quit on day one — they fade out in weeks three to six, when follow-up depends on coaches finding time they don't have. Static PDF programs can't respond when a member is travelling, injured or short on time, and drop-off is usually noticed only when a renewal doesn't happen.
>
> JiXora closes that gap. Members complete a guided intake and readiness questionnaire, and JiXora builds their 13-week plan from the coach's own program templates. Automated check-ins go out on each member's schedule, and free-text replies — "travelling this week", "my knee is sore" — are interpreted and attached to the member record. When a reply changes what a member can do, JiXora drafts a specific adjustment for the coach to approve. Reported pain is never auto-progressed.
>
> Coaches work from one console: drop-off risk flags, a review queue of drafted changes, nutrition and habit trends, and a full timeline for every member's day-90 review.
>
> JiXora is architected as a serverless application on Amazon Web Services and is currently in early access. Coaches and fitness businesses can request access at jixora.space.
