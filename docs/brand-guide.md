# JiXora Brand Guide

## Positioning statement

For coaches, gyms and corporate wellness providers who lose members in the first
weeks of a program, **JiXora** is AI program delivery and accountability software
that builds personalised 90-day plans, runs automated daily check-ins and flags
drop-off risk to coaches — so more members finish what they started, without
coaches adding hours to their week.

- **Category:** B2B SaaS — fitness coaching and member-retention software
- **Buyer:** gym/studio owners, head coaches, independent coaches, wellness program managers
- **User:** coaches (console) and their members (intake and check-ins)
- **Primary CTA:** Request early access · **Secondary:** Explore the coach console

## Logo

The mark is a progress ring: a volt arc covering 270° of a ring (three quarters of
a program completed) with a paper-white “check-in” dot at the centre, on a rounded
Pine 900 square. Files: `public/favicon.svg`, `public/brand/logo.svg`,
`public/brand/logo-on-dark.svg`, `public/brand/logo-512.png`.

Clear space: at least half the mark's width on every side. Do not recolour the arc,
rotate the mark or place it on busy imagery.

## Typography

- **Display:** Bricolage Grotesque (variable), semibold, tight tracking (-0.02 to -0.03em)
- **Body/UI:** Inter (variable)
- **Data & labels:** JetBrains Mono, uppercase with wide tracking for eyebrows

All fonts are self-hosted via Fontsource — no third-party font requests.

## Interface language

- Light, paper-toned marketing surfaces; product UI on deep pine “screens”.
- 1.25 rem card radius, 1 px hairline borders (`#DCDBD0`), shadows only on elevated product mock-ups.
- Status is always colour **and** text (never colour alone).
- Motion: 700 ms ease-out rise on scroll reveal, 200 ms hover transitions; all motion disabled under `prefers-reduced-motion`.

## Voice

Direct, specific, coach-to-coach. Describe what the product does for a member on a
given day rather than abstract AI claims. Never claim traction, customers or
outcomes that are not verified.
